Text Messaging and Mobile Opt In

EquityNest Capital offers text messaging to consumers who request information about our loan programs or who submit a loan inquiry or application to us. Participation is entirely optional and is never a condition of applying for, or receiving, any loan or other service.

How you consent

You consent to receive text messages from EquityNest Capital by checking the consent box on one of our lead forms or loan applications, by submitting your phone number to us through one of our advertisements, or by texting us first. Consent is given by you affirmatively. We do not pre check consent boxes and we do not bundle text message consent with acceptance of any other agreement.

What we send

Messages relate to your loan inquiry or application. They include follow up from your assigned loan officer, requests for documents needed to underwrite your file, status updates on your application, and appointment confirmations. Message frequency varies depending on the stage of your file. Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further messages. You can rejoin at any time by replying START. For help, reply HELP, call us at (281) 270-9600, or email info@equitynestcapital.com.

Your mobile information is not shared or sold

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Your phone number and your text messaging consent are used solely by EquityNest Capital to communicate with you about your inquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers who help us deliver messages, such as our messaging platform and our customer relationship management system. Those providers act on our instructions, may use the information only to provide the service to us, and may not use it for their own marketing.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
