Airbnb and Short-Term Rental DSCR Loans: Everything You Need to Know

EquityNest Capital April 29, 2026 11 min read

The short-term rental market has reshaped real estate investing. In the right market, a single-family home listed on Airbnb or VRBO can generate two, three, or even four times the monthly income of a traditional long-term rental. That economic reality has created a growing class of investors who want to build STR portfolios, but the financing options haven't always kept pace with the opportunity.

Most conventional lenders simply don't know how to underwrite short-term rental income. They default to long-term rental comparables or, worse, they reject the deal outright because the income stream looks unfamiliar. This is where STR-specific DSCR loans come in, and where working with a broker who specializes in this space makes all the difference.

This guide covers how Airbnb and short-term rental DSCR loans work, how lenders calculate STR income, what you need to qualify, which markets perform best for STR investors, and how to get started.

What Is an Airbnb DSCR Loan?

An Airbnb DSCR loan is a non-QM investment property mortgage that qualifies the borrower based on the short-term rental income of the property rather than the borrower's personal income. Like all DSCR loans, it bypasses W-2s, tax returns, and personal income verification entirely. The question the lender asks is simple: does this property generate enough rental income to cover its debt service?

The key difference between a standard DSCR loan and an STR-specific DSCR loan is how income is calculated. Long-term rental DSCR loans use current or market lease rates to establish income. STR DSCR loans use short-term rental data: either the property's own booking history if it's an operating STR, or projected STR income from market analysis tools if the property is new to the STR market or being purchased for the first time.

These loans are made to investors for investment properties only. They're business-purpose loans, which is what allows lenders to use alternative income documentation without running afoul of consumer mortgage regulations. The property must be used as a rental, not as the borrower's primary or secondary residence.

STR properties in strong vacation markets can generate significantly more monthly income than long-term rentals, which often allows investors to qualify for larger loan amounts or more easily meet DSCR minimums than the same property would achieve as a traditional rental.

How STR Income Is Calculated for DSCR

This is where STR DSCR loans get nuanced, and where an experienced broker adds real value. Lenders approach STR income documentation in a few different ways depending on the property's history and the lender's guidelines:

  • Active Airbnb or VRBO listing: lenders typically use 12 months of actual booking history, often obtained through the platform's earnings summary
  • New purchase with no STR history: lenders use projected STR income from tools like AirDNA, Rabbu, or STR Insights, which provide market-level data on occupancy rates and average daily rates for similar properties
  • Some lenders blend projected and comparable data, or require a market analysis report from the appraiser
  • Occupancy rate is typically applied to the potential gross income to arrive at a realistic annual income figure, which is then divided by 12 for the monthly income used in the DSCR calculation

The market you're investing in matters significantly. Lenders are more comfortable with STR income in established vacation markets with deep data sets and consistent demand. Emerging markets with limited comparables can be harder to underwrite, though experienced brokers know which lenders have the most flexibility on this.

One important note: lenders typically apply a vacancy factor and sometimes a management fee assumption to the gross STR income. The resulting net income is what goes into the DSCR calculation. Understanding how your lender arrives at that number is important for structuring your deal correctly upfront.

Requirements for STR and Airbnb DSCR Loans

Requirements vary by lender, but here's what most STR DSCR programs ask for:

  • Credit score: 680 minimum is common for STR programs, with better pricing at 720 and above
  • Down payment: 20-25% is typical; some programs allow 15% with stronger compensating factors
  • DSCR: most lenders want 1.0 or higher using STR income; some programs allow as low as 0.75
  • Property type: single-family homes, condos (warrantable and sometimes non-warrantable), and small multifamily
  • Reserves: 6-12 months of mortgage payments in accessible liquid accounts
  • Market: most lenders prefer established STR markets with available AirDNA or comparable data
  • HOA: the property's HOA (if applicable) must permit short-term rentals
  • Local regulations: the market must allow short-term rentals under local zoning and ordinances

That last point is critical. Before pursuing STR financing on a specific property, confirm that short-term rentals are permitted by local law and, if applicable, by the HOA governing documents. Municipalities from Honolulu to New York City have implemented significant restrictions on STRs, and buying into a market without understanding the regulatory landscape is a risk that no lender can protect you from.

How to Apply for an STR DSCR Loan

The process for an STR DSCR loan follows the same general flow as other DSCR loans, with a few additional steps related to income documentation:

  • Identify the property and confirm local regulations permit short-term rentals
  • If purchasing, pull AirDNA or comparable STR market data to establish projected income
  • If the property is an active STR, pull 12 months of Airbnb or VRBO earnings history
  • Confirm your credit, down payment, and reserves are in place
  • Work with a non-QM broker familiar with STR lending to identify the right lender for your market and deal size
  • Submit the application, including the STR income documentation
  • Order the appraisal, which on some programs includes an STR income analysis
  • Complete underwriting and close, typically in 21-35 days

Having a broker who knows which lenders are actively pricing STR deals in your market is the single biggest time-saver in this process. STR guidelines are more lender-specific than standard DSCR programs, and the wrong lender can mean a 30-day delay or an unnecessary denial on a perfectly fundable deal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use an STR DSCR loan for a property I've never rented before?
Yes. For new purchases or properties without an established STR rental history, most lenders accept projected STR income from tools like AirDNA. The appraiser may also provide an STR income opinion. You don't need an active Airbnb listing to qualify, though having one with documented income history obviously strengthens the file.
Do I need to already have STR experience to qualify?
Not typically. STR DSCR loans are available to first-time STR investors. Some lenders may apply a more conservative income estimate or require a slightly higher down payment for borrowers with no STR track record, but the lack of prior STR experience is not disqualifying. The property's income potential is the primary underwriting factor.
What if my target market restricts short-term rentals?
If the property is in a market where short-term rentals are prohibited or heavily restricted, most lenders will not fund it as an STR. You may still be able to finance it as a standard DSCR loan using long-term rental income, but the STR income advantage is gone. Always verify local regulations before structuring a deal around STR income.
Are STR DSCR rates higher than regular DSCR rates?
They can be slightly higher, typically 0.25-0.50% above a comparable long-term rental DSCR loan. This reflects the perceived variability in STR income relative to a long-term lease. However, because STR properties often generate significantly more income than long-term rentals, the higher rate is frequently offset by a stronger DSCR ratio, which can actually result in better overall terms.
